Plain-language policy · September 9, 2026

Privacy

Berkeley Trash Day is designed to work without knowing who you are or exactly where you live. The report form asks only for a 9th Street block, one or more collection types, and an observed pickup day.

What a report stores

  • The selected block range
  • The selected combination of trash, recycling, and compost
  • The reported collection day
  • The time the report was received

What we do not ask for

The project does not ask for your name, email, phone number, account, exact address, photo, or location coordinates. Please do not submit personal information through the service.

Technical data

The hosting provider may temporarily process standard connection data, such as an IP address, to deliver the site and enforce abuse limits. The application does not place that information in its community-report database.

The interactive map loads map tiles and style resources from OpenFreeMap. Like other web hosts, that provider receives ordinary connection data such as your IP address and browser request headers when those map resources load. Search text stays in your browser and is not sent to a third-party geocoder.

Retention and purpose

Reports contribute to the schedule for 180 days. They are used only to calculate block-level community agreement and maintain the service. The project does not sell community-report data.

Unofficial community information

Berkeley Trash Day is an independent, open-source community project and is not affiliated with the City of Berkeley. For an official schedule, call Berkeley Zero Waste at (510) 981-7270 or use the City of Berkeley website.
