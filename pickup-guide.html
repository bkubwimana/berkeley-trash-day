West Berkeley collection guide

Trash, recycling and compost pickup days

Search a West Berkeley street range to see the pickup days neighbors have reported for trash, recycling and compost. Multiple cart types can be collected on the same day, and the tracker shows the report count behind every result.

Search your street Report a pickup day

How the community schedule works

Choose a supported street range in the map or search box. The weekly calendar keeps trash, recycling and compost separate, even when two or all three share a collection day. Results come from recent resident reports and expire after 180 days so old observations do not remain indefinitely.

Add a pickup reminder to your calendar

When a collection day has community consensus, the tracker can download a calendar file with 26 weekly reminders. The file is generated in your browser; downloading it does not create an account or send a calendar event to the server.

Community information, not an official schedule

Collection days can change for holidays or service conditions. Confirm important dates with Berkeley Zero Waste at (510) 981-7270 or the City of Berkeley residential waste page.

Help make the result useful

If you observed or reasonably confirmed a pickup, submit the street range, every cart type collected that day, and the weekday. The project does not ask for your name, account, exact address, photo or location coordinates.
