# Research: Friendly Weekly Calendar

## Decision 1: Show a typical week, not dated or recurring events

The API contains weekdays and community evidence, but no authoritative holiday exceptions. A seven-day undated week provides immediate value without implying a guaranteed municipal date. External recurring calendar export is deferred until holiday exceptions have a reliable source.

## Decision 2: Use light civic styling as the default

Warm off-white surfaces, dark text, and restrained forest-green accents make the utility feel approachable while retaining the existing community identity. Recycling remains blue, compost green, and trash neutral for scanability.

## Decision 3: Use the selected generated calendar logo

The first generated asset visibly joins a cart, recycling loop, and calendar. It communicates the product better than initials or the more abstract second draft. The transparent PNG is stored with the public project assets.

## Decision 4: Keep attribution, reduce visual competition

OpenStreetMap remains credited because its street data validated corridor labels. Moving the credit to the footer preserves attribution while allowing the schematic and calendar to carry the primary visual hierarchy.
