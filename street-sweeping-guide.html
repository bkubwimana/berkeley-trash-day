West Berkeley parking guide

Berkeley street sweeping schedules

Search a West Berkeley street range and choose the curb side where you park. The tracker matches the range to the City of Berkeley's published residential street sweeping tables and can generate a calendar reminder.

Why the curb side matters

Opposite sides of a Berkeley street can have different sweeping days. The tracker labels sides by odd or even addresses and compass direction when the City source provides that information. Select the side where the vehicle is actually parked.

Add a parking reminder

A side-specific calendar file includes the published recurrence and safety notes. It is a convenience reminder, not a parking authorization. The City says streets are not swept on City holidays and resume on the next regularly scheduled day.

Posted signs always control

Read the signs at the vehicle before parking or relying on a reminder. Temporary restrictions and source changes may not appear here. See the official City of Berkeley street sweeping page for the source schedules.

What the tracker covers

The lookup currently focuses on supported street ranges in West Berkeley. Some ranges have no matching residential schedule, and commercial sweeping or other parking restrictions may follow different rules. When no reviewed match exists, the tracker says so instead of guessing.
